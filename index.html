3 · SCADA, telemetry and network architecture

This matches the PDF's SCADA & Telemetry concept (page 8): field devices send measurements to supervisory systems for monitoring, alarms and reporting.

Sensor → PLC/RTU

Measurements are converted to electrical or digital signals. The controller reads I/O and applies local logic.

PLC/RTU → SCADA

An approved industrial protocol and secured gateway transport selected tags, device health and alarms.

SCADA → Operator

The HMI displays trends, alarms and context. Operators verify conditions and follow approved procedures.

4 · Wastewater and environmental return pathway

Separate from potable-water treatment: wastewater is collected by municipal sewer systems, treated, then discharged or reused according to quality and permit requirements.

5 · Image library: dams, infrastructure and treatment

Illustrative educational photos. For official Umgeni site-specific images, open the organisation's gallery.
Browse official gallery ↗

Dams and reservoirs

Store raw water, buffer seasonal changes and support controlled abstraction. Rainfall alone does not determine safe yield; inflow, evaporation, demand and operating rules also matter.

Catchments and rivers

Catchment rainfall and land conditions influence inflows and raw-water quality. Monitoring can help teams investigate turbidity spikes or changing river conditions.

Control and instrumentation

Flow meters, pressure transmitters, level sensors, analyzers and motor feedback can feed PLC/RTU systems and the SCADA display when properly integrated.

Pumps and pipelines

Pumps add energy to move water where gravity is insufficient. Valves, pressure, flow and pump condition are important operating indicators.

Water quality testing

Online instruments support rapid monitoring, while laboratory sampling and accredited methods are needed for parameters that cannot be reliably inferred from a dashboard alone.

Bulk supply and communities

The bulk water system supplies water service authorities; municipal networks typically handle the final distribution to local areas and households.
